To help route your message, please use one of the subject lines below.

Calculator error reports

Subject: “Calculator Bug Report” — if a result looks wrong, include the calculator name and the exact numbers you entered so the problem can be reproduced and added to the test suite.

Content corrections

Subject: “Content Correction” — include the page link and, if you can, a link to the source for the correct information. See the Editorial Policy for how corrections are handled.

General questions and suggestions

Subject: “General Question” — anything about the site, or ideas for new calculators and guides.

Privacy requests

Subject: “Privacy Request” — questions about data handling or requests related to our Privacy Policy.

CalcMyCard can’t give personal financial advice or help with a specific card account — for that, contact your card issuer or a nonprofit credit counselor.
