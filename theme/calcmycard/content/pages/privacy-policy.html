This Privacy Policy explains what information CalcMyCard ("we," "us," or "this site") collects when you visit, and how it's used.

Calculator inputs

The numbers you enter into any calculator on this site (balances, interest rates, payments, etc.) are processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. They are never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns — which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and general geographic and device information. This data is not used to identify you individually.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertisements served by third-party providers, including Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Third-party links

This site may link to third-party websites (for example, sources cited in our guides). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalized advertising using the links above. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
